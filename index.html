Privacy & local storage

Notes and scan photos are encrypted on this device. Your password is sent securely to the existing VELTHON sign-in service to verify access, and also unlocks your local notes. First online sign-in is required before offline unlock. Keep an encrypted backup before clearing browser data. Sign-in and security events are retained by the existing service for up to 365 days. Cloud saving uses your device’s Files/share picker; direct account sync is not connected.
